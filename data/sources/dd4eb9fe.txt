Semi-Skilled Aircraft Fitters
Broughton, Wales
Have you got at least 12 months experience in a manufacturing role? Then we welcome you to join our
Talent Community for the Airbus team in Broughton.
What you can expect
As a Semi-Skilled Aircraft Fitter, you will be working as part of the aircraft wing build production team to support with the assembly and installation of components, including repair of metallic and composite materials.
This role requires flexibility and movement between various working environments in confined spaces, and on occasion, at height.
↓ Discover a shift that works for you by clicking below.
Shifts: Double Days
Join us on a double-day shift, working 
Monday to Friday
 with alternating weekly shifts from ** 6:00am–1:00pm** and 
1:30pm–8:40pm
. Enjoy a competitive pay rate of 
£40,294.
Please note that a shift pattern will be allocated to you, flexibility is a must.
Shifts: Nights
Looking for a structured night shift role? Work 
Monday to Thursday
, 
8:45pm–6:00am
, with three days off and earn a highly competitive salary of ** £43,652**.
Please note that a shift pattern will be allocated to you, flexibility is a must.
 
What we're looking for
Semi-Skilled candidates are required to have a minimum of 12 months manufacturing experience.
Preferably we are looking for candidates with an NVQ Level 3 or similar experience in an engineering discipline.
You must be able to demonstrate the following:
•
Physical fitness (flexibility to work in different environments, heights and confined spaces)
•
Health, Safety and Human Factors awareness
•
Team skills, innovative thinking, and good communication

Working at Airbus
At Airbus' Broughton site, they manufacture the wings for all Airbus' commercial aircraft, with the exception of the A220, which sustain toughly 9,000 UK jobs.
It's home to a workforce of some 6,000 highly skilled personnel, and has a proud tradition of aerospace manufacturing, dating back 80 years, having produced such aviation classics as the Wellington and Lancaster, and De Haviland's Mosquito and Comet.
Broughton is conveniently located in close proximity to major road networks – specifically the A55 and M53. Distances to surround cities:
Chester: 7 miles, Wrexham: 10 miles, Liverpool: 18 miles, Warrington: 22 miles.
