Valladolid: Intrum workers call 24‑hour strikes on 21 May and 10 June
Last Updated: June 11, 2026 at 5:01 PM
Low Business services (debt collection/call center)
UGT has called a campaign of mobilisations against Intrum in Valladolid: partial stoppages from 30 April 2026 and two 24-hour strikes on 21 May and 10 June 2026. The union denounces the deterioration of working conditions, the removal of remote work, and serious technical issues that particularly affect the Valladolid centre.
This alert was originally created on 5 May 2026.
Effective Dates
Thursday, 21 May 2026
