What kind of job can I get in the Digital & IT department?
Our Digital & IT department recruits a wide range of profiles, such as Software engineers, Networks specialists, Data analysts, Artificial intelligence professionals, Digital project managers, IT Product owners, Cybersecurity experts
How to apply for a job in Digital & IT at Airbus ?
You will find all open positions on our job board, so it's easy to apply online. If you need some help to get ready to apply, you can learn more information about our recruitment process.
What are the benefits of working at Airbus in Digital & IT?
You will get the unique opportunity to work in a dynamic department and to shape the way we tackle digital & IT matters in the future. At Airbus, we believe in mentorships programmes and we provide plenty of opportunities to grow your career and get inspired everyday. We also set you up for a competitive salary and a wide range of benefits, such as meal participation, day care for your children and health care for you and your family.
