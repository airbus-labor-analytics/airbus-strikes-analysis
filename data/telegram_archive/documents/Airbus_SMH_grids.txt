Airbus SMH grids
This page presents 
the 2025 and 2026 Hierarchical Minimum Salary (SMH) grids applicable to all Airbus Group employees in France.
Contrary to what Airbus management announced in its March 9, 2026 communication to all employees in France, the 2026 Airbus SMH salary grid has not changed following the Airbus Group negotiations that ended on February 26, 2026, with the signing of the corresponding agreement by FO, CFE-CGC, CFTC, and CFDT, and which was not signed by the CGT.
The Airbus 2025 SMH grid results from 
salary policy agreement at Airbus group level in France of March 21, 2024
https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/acco/id/ACCOTEXT000049593263
*, and is applicable to all Airbus employees from September 1, 2024, to December 31, 2025.
The Airbus 2026 SMH grid results from 
salary policy agreement at Airbus group level in France of March 26, 2025
https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/acco/id/ACCOTEXT000051618815
*, and is applicable to all Airbus employees from January 1, 2026.
*signed by FO, CFE-CGC, CFTC, and CFDT and not signed by the CGT.
The rules for applying these SMH grids come from the 
Airbus Group's RELOAD agreement of February 6, 2023
https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/acco/id/ACCOTEXT000048899587
 (signed by FO, CFE-CGC, and CFTC and not signed by CGT), which itself stems from the new 
National Collective Agreement for the Steel Industry of February 7, 2022
https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALICONT000046993250
 (signed by FO, CFE-CGC, and CFDT and not signed by CGT).
For more information, please consult the text of the agreements mentioned above.
Airbus Annual SMH Grids
The annual SMHs in the grids below are given as gross annual amounts and include all remuneration components, excluding seniority bonuses, variable pay for executives, bonuses or increments, salary compensations (e.g., team, on-call duty), premiums and gratifications, employee savings schemes, profit-sharing, and participation schemes.
Please note that Monthly SMH = Annual SMH / 13
CGT Opinion:
 The RELOAD agreement defines the principle of an annual remuneration spread over 13 months, which lowers the monthly SMHs. This allows for Airbus monthly SMHs to be lower than Metallurgy monthly SMHs calculated over 12 months! 
Annual SMH Grid 2025 applicable from September 1, 2024, to December 31, 2025
 
Annual SMH Grid 2026 applicable from January 1, 2026
Airbus Annual SMH Grids for Employees Under 27
CGT Opinion:
 For employees under 27, the agreement considers that for the same job class (F11 or F12), and thus for equal work, young employees have fewer rights than older employees in terms of salary… 
Airbus Annual SMH Grid 2025 for employees under 27, applicable from September 1, 2024, to December 31, 2025
 
Airbus Annual SMH Grid 2026 for employees under 27, applicable from January 1, 2026
When is my salary set to the SMH?
The immediate adjustment of the base salary to the monthly SMH occurs only in three cases: hiring, promotion to a higher job class, or change in working hours (transition to hourly fixed rate or annual fixed rate in days).
What happens if my annual remuneration is below the SMH?
If your annual remuneration is below the annual SMH, an « annual remuneration supplement » is paid as a differential allowance on your payslip before the end of May of the year following the reference calendar year. Annual remuneration includes all remuneration components defined above.
CGT Opinion:
 This allowance is not considered salary and therefore is not a vested right. It is reduced or lost if you move to a lower job class (made possible by the RELOAD agreement, for example, in the case of a reorganization). Furthermore, this allowance will be « eroded » by the amount of any salary increase (AG + AI) when the latter is lower than the differential allowance, your total remuneration (salary + differential allowance) will then remain unchanged.
It is for these reasons that the CGT consistently advocates for an automatic salary adjustment to the SMH as soon as the salary falls below the SMH.
