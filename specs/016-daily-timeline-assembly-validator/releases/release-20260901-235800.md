## Summary of Changes

### 1. Madrid Timezone-Aware Timeline Freshness Validator (`src/validate_timeline_freshness.py`)
- Evaluates freshness of the strike timeline relative to `Europe/Madrid` (CEST) calendar date.
- Emits four standard operational states: `UP_TO_DATE` (Green), `PENDING_TODAY` (Amber), `WEEKEND_PAUSE` (Blue), and `STALE_ALERT` (Red).
- Enforces strict chronological monotonicity and validates the presence of linked primary source documents.

### 2. Complete Conflict Chronology & Factory Assembly Minutes (`data/conflict_metrics.json`, `src/analysis_engine.py`)
- Up-to-date chronogram containing 21 continuous milestones from 2021 through September 1, 2026 (including the Aug 31 extraordinary SIMA session and Sep 1 multi-plant assemblies).
- 12 assembly milestones cross-linked to verified source minutes in `data/telegram_archive/assembly_minutes/`.

### 3. Interactive UI & Telemetry HUD (`dashboard/index.html`, `dashboard/app.js`, `dashboard/js/modules/union_force.js`)
- Dynamic Alert Banner (`#timeline-freshness-banner`) in `#sec-unions-timeline` with direct links to Telegram channel and live synchronization.
- Floating Dynamic Island HUD pill (`#hud-timeline-freshness`) reflecting the live status code.
- Interactive filter buttons by factory site (`Getafe`, `Illescas`, `San Pablo`, `Tablada`, `CBC El Puerto`, `Albacete`, `Barajas`) and actor type (`Asambleas`, `SIMA`, `Sindicatos`, `Empresa`).
- Integrated document reader modal for viewing unedited transcripts of assembly minutes.
