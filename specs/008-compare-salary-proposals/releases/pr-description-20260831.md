## Summary
Implements an interactive multi-proposal gross annual wage simulator, a 10-dimension comparative bargaining matrix, and a 4-series Chart.js salary projection engine. Workers can dynamically compare 5-year nominal and real (CPI-deflated) salary trajectories between the **Airbus SE Company Offer**, the **CGT Assembly Platform**, and the **Strike Committee 11-Point Platform** (presented at SIMA on 27/08/2026).

## Specification
- **User Story 1 (MVP)**: Interactive 5-year simulation of gross annual nominal and real earnings across 3 proposals with two-way slider reactivity.
- **User Story 2**: Exhaustive 10-dimension comparison matrix covering base salary, arrears, revision clause, telework, working hours, employment guarantees, seniority, healthcare, strike sanctions, and union oversight.
- **User Story 3**: 4-series Chart.js visualization (`wagesChart`) and 5-year cumulative differential KPI cards (+46.2k€ for CGT, +43.6k€ for Strike Committee vs. Company Offer).

## Implementation
- **Analytical Core (`src/analysis_engine.py`)**: `get_salary_proposals_comparison(base_salary, cpi_rate)` calculating year-by-year compounding, deflated purchasing power, cumulative totals, and proposal deltas.
- **Client Simulator (`dashboard/app.js`)**: Real-time `calculateSalaryProposals(baseSalary, ipcRate)` and `renderSalaryProposalsMatrix()` dynamic table injector.
- **Interactive UI (`dashboard/index.html`)**: 3-proposal comparison table, 5-year differential summary KPI cards, and Sub-Section 5 bargaining matrix table.
- **Datasets (`data/conflict_metrics.json`, `dashboard/data.js`)**: Canonical proposal definitions, comparison matrix items, and fallback offline fixtures.
